About Golf Scheduler

Golf Scheduler is a simple, modern tool designed to help leagues organize play months, play days, and player availability with ease. Whether you're managing a small local league or coordinating a larger group, the system keeps everything clear, consistent, and easy to update.

Players can log in, view or update their golf schedule, update availability, and stay connected with their league. Administrators have access to powerful tools for managing leagues, users, and schedules — all from a clean, intuitive interface.

This project is built with Node.js, Express, HTML, Javascript, SQLite, and a focus on clean UI design and maintainable code.
